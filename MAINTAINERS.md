## Science Leads

Science Leads provide subject-matter expertise and guidance on the scientific methods, algorithms, and assumptions implemented in WindNinja.

Science Leads should be consulted on changes that materially affect the scientific behavior or interpretation of WindNinja.

## Roles and Authority

The roles above have different areas of responsibility:

* **Project Lead:** Provides overall project and technical leadership and makes final decisions when needed.
* **Software Maintainers:** Have authority over day-to-day software development, code review, and maintenance.
* **Science Leads:** Provide scientific guidance and review for changes affecting WindNinja's scientific methods or results.
* **Contributors:** Propose and implement changes through the project's contribution process.

A person may serve in more than one role.

## Changes to Maintainers and Leads

Project leadership may add or remove maintainers and leads as project needs change. Changes to this file should be made through a pull request.
